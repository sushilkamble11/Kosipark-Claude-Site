# Kosipark V2

This project is the streamlined public website for Kosciuszko Tourist Park.

- V1 remains in `../site` and must not be modified from this project.
- V2 does not collect guest details, keep a cart, take payment, or create reservations.
- The homepage sends dates and guest counts to GuestPoint's hosted secure booking page.
- `/book/calendar` may use read-only GuestPoint availability and pricing data for planning and genuine recommendations.
- Never invent scarcity, savings, availability, or a held reservation.
- Do not deploy without the user's explicit approval.
