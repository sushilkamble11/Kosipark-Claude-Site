Welcome to Kosciuszko Tourist Park

Your gateway to the Snowy Mountains

Nestled within the serene Kosciuszko National Park, our park is a paradise for nature lovers and outdoor adventurers. A short drive from Australia's highest peak, we offer everything from Heritage Chalets to camping sites, all set in the tranquility of natural bushland.

Whether you're here for skiing at Perisher Valley, 14 kilometres away, or exploring the alpine landscapes in summer, the park is the perfect base — with a guest lounge, camp kitchen and drying room, surrounded by snowgum forest and native wildlife.

Guest Kitchen & Lounge

A wood fire to come back to

Our large guest kitchen and lounge has two stoves with ovens, a microwave, sink, fridge, dining furniture, lounge chairs and a free gas barbecue — all warmed by a cosy wood fire.

  • Kiosk and two amenity blocks with hot showers
  • Laundry with coin-operated washers and dryers
  • Undercover wash-up area, drying room and playground
  • Picnic area and three walking trails from the park
