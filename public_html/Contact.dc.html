Getting Here

On the road between Jindabyne and Perisher

Follow the Kosciuszko Road out of Jindabyne towards Perisher; the park is on the left at Sawpit Creek, inside the national park boundary. Vehicle entry fees apply — ask reception about park passes. Snow chains may be required in winter.
