Age restrictions & parental consent

The lead guest must be 18 or older at check-in. Guests under 18 must be accompanied by a parent or legal guardian throughout their stay. If a minor stays without their parent or guardian, we require written email consent before arrival, including the parent's full name and contact details, confirmation they authorise the stay, the name of the supervising adult, and acknowledgment of these terms. We reserve the right to refuse check-in or cancel without refund if consent is not provided.

Deposit & payment — standard rates

The deposit is 50% of the total booking or one full night's tariff, whichever is higher. Booking 7 or more days before arrival, the deposit is taken at booking and the balance charged 8 days prior. Booking within 7 days of arrival, full payment is taken at booking and is non-refundable. If the balance payment fails you have 24 hours to resolve it; if unresolved the booking is cancelled and all amounts paid are forfeited. Card payments incur a 2% surcharge.

Promotional, discounted & Stay-and-Save rates

Full payment is required at booking. These rates are non-refundable and non-amendable under any circumstances.

Minimum stay requirements

  • Winter weekends (June–September, any stay including a Friday, Saturday or Sunday night): 2-night minimum.
  • 20 December – 5 January: 3-night minimum.
  • Easter and NSW winter school holidays: 2-night minimum.
  • Snow season runs from the June long weekend through 30 September.

Amendment policy

Submit all amendment requests through our online form only. Submitting the form does not confirm any change — your booking remains under the original terms until you receive written confirmation from us.

Outside snow season (October – early June)

  • 15+ days: free like-for-like amendment (same rate type and accommodation) if available.
  • 8–14 days: transfer fee of the greater of $50, 10% of total tariff, or one full night's tariff.
  • Under 7 days: no amendments permitted.

Snow season (June long weekend – 30 September)

  • No free amendments at any time.
  • Date moves permitted within the same snow season only, subject to availability, with a transfer fee of the greater of $50, 10% of total tariff, or one full night's tariff.
  • No amendments within 14 days of arrival. Bookings cannot be moved to a different season or the following year.
  • Chalets and cabins: Saturday check-in or check-out is not permitted during snow season.

Any amendment that reduces the total booking value or stay length will be treated as a cancellation, and cancellation fees will apply.

Site allocation

Specific sites may be requested but are not guaranteed. We will do our best to accommodate your request. Allocation is at management discretion and may change up to arrival.

Equipment details

You must declare the type and dimensions of your caravan, motorhome, camper trailer, van or tent, including drawbar length. Sites are allocated on that basis. If your equipment differs materially from what was declared we may be unable to accommodate you, and no refund applies. Awnings are not permitted on motorhome sites due to site width.

Rate plans

More than one rate plan may be offered for the same accommodation, each with its own deposit, cancellation and amendment conditions. The conditions shown against the rate you select at the time of booking prevail over the general terms above.

Incomplete bookings

If you enter your contact details but do not complete a booking, we may retain those details and contact you once about your enquiry. You can opt out at any time by replying to that message or emailing stay@kosipark.com.au.

Occupancy & guest count

Maximum occupancy applies to all guests including adults, children (2–15) and infants (under 2). Infants are free but count toward the limit. Unapproved extra guests or exceeding the limit may result in additional charges and/or eviction without refund.

Parking & vehicles

One vehicle per site or cabin is included. Additional vehicles are $20 per night, registered at reception on check-in. All vehicles must be registered at the office with make, model, colour and registration number. Park only in designated areas; the speed limit is 10 km/h throughout the park. Oversized or unroadworthy vehicles require prior approval. An automated boom gate is coming soon — only registered vehicles will be granted access.

Electric vehicle charging

Charging is only permitted where the EV charging surcharge has been selected and paid. Guests must use the power supply allocated to their accommodation or powered site and provide their own safe, suitable charging cable and extension lead — no separate charger, power point or charging bay is provided. Charging is subject to available electrical capacity and may be stopped at our discretion if it creates a safety risk or overload.

Drying room

Drying room access is $5 per person per night and must be added for the full duration of your booking at the time of booking or amendment. It cannot be added for selected nights only. To add access, email stay@kosipark.com.au or use our online form.

Check-in & check-out

Check-in is from 3pm and check-out by 10am. Early check-in before 1pm is $30 and late checkout until midday is $30; requests must be made at least 24 hours in advance and are subject to availability.

Failure to vacate by 10am without prior arrangement is charged as one additional night at the standard nightly rate; after midday, an additional full night. If the site or cabin is required for incoming guests and you have not vacated, you will be charged and asked to leave immediately. Leave your cabin or site clean — remove all rubbish, wash and return all dishes, and report any damage to reception. Excessive cleaning may result in bond deductions.

Firewood & fires

Premium seasoned firewood is $20 per bag, available at reception during office hours. Collecting or burning timber from Kosciuszko National Park is illegal under the National Parks and Wildlife Act and carries substantial fines. Use designated firepits only, never leave fires unattended, and extinguish them completely before leaving. During total fire bans or extreme fire danger (typically November to April) all firepits are closed — check current restrictions at rfs.nsw.gov.au before lighting any fire. Breaches may result in fines, eviction and liability for firefighting costs.

Pets

Pets are strictly prohibited in all accommodation and throughout the park, in accordance with National Parks and Wildlife Service regulations. Breach of this policy may result in immediate cancellation, removal from the premises and forfeiture of all monies paid including the security bond.

Quiet hours

Quiet hours are 10pm to 6am — no loud music, voices or generators, and minimal vehicle movements. First breach: warning. Second breach: $300 fine. Continued breaches: eviction without refund.

Wi-Fi & mobile coverage

Telstra coverage is excellent via our on-site tower; other carriers may have limited or no coverage in the alpine region. Wi-Fi is provided via paid vouchers at reception and relies on satellite and shared backhaul links, so speeds may vary during peak times.

Health, safety & the alpine environment

Follow all government and park protocols. Visibly ill guests may be required to leave at their own cost. No smoking or vaping indoors, and emergency contact details must be provided at check-in.

Conditions change rapidly — snow, wind and rain can occur year-round. Walking paths may include uneven surfaces, rocks and steep areas. Observe native animals, snakes and insects from a distance, and do not feed or approach them. The nearest hospital is Cooma, approximately 60 km away. Guests are responsible for their own safety during outdoor activities and should bring appropriate clothing and equipment. In an emergency, dial 000.

Security bond, damage & CCTV

A $100 bond is charged at check-in for cabins, refunded within 7–14 days after departure provided there is no damage, excessive cleaning, missing items, policy breaches or unpaid charges. We will retain the bond and invoice any costs exceeding it. You are responsible for any damage, loss, excessive cleaning or missing items caused during your stay. CCTV operates in public areas for safety and security; by staying you consent to CCTV monitoring. We are not liable for third-party acts or theft of personal belongings.

Park facilities & lost property

Follow all signs and staff directions, and use facilities at your own risk. Label all food in the camp kitchen fridge with your name and departure date — unlabelled food will be discarded without notice, and all items must be removed on checkout. Damage to facilities may be charged and deducted from your security bond.

We are not responsible for loss, theft or damage to personal items. Perishables are disposed of immediately; non-perishables are held at reception for 7 days, then donated to local charities.

National park compliance

Kosciuszko Tourist Park operates within Kosciuszko National Park under the National Parks and Wildlife Act 1974 (NSW). By staying with us you agree to comply with all NPWS regulations; respect and protect native flora, fauna and natural features; not feed, harass or disturb wildlife; not remove or damage plants, rocks or natural materials; report any damage to park property; and follow all park signage and ranger directions. Vehicle entry fees apply and are the guest's responsibility — check current rates at nationalparks.nsw.gov.au. Breaches may result in NPWS fines, eviction without refund and legal action by NPWS.
