Walking tracks guide ↗

Mount Kosciuszko Walk

The closest you can stay to Charlotte Pass. Hike or ride from Charlotte Pass to the summit of Mount Kosciuszko, through the high peaks of the national park.

Kosciuszko Education Centre

Located 200 metres from our park, the centre offers education opportunities for all ages to learn about Australian wildlife.

Winter

Fourteen kilometres from the lifts

Perisher Ski Resort

We are the closest caravan park to Perisher, making us a perfect location for skiers. Perisher is the largest ski resort in the Southern Hemisphere.

Gondola & Chairlift

A scenic chairlift ride is a must-do in summer too — fifteen minutes one way, rising 560 vertical metres to the start of Thredbo's alpine hiking tracks.

Thredbo Leisure Centre

Five minutes' walk from Thredbo Village Square: gym, pool, waterslide, sports hall, bouldering wall, squash court and trampoline centre.

Summer & Adventure

Long days on the trails

Thredbo Mountain Bike Park

Australia's premier mountain bike park: over 40 km of lift-accessed gravity trails, skills parks, cross country trails, two pump tracks and an MTB school.

Thredbo Alpine Coaster

The Southern Hemisphere's first year-round alpine coaster: 1.5 km of uphill and downhill track with a tunnel, bridges and speeds up to 40 km/h.

Horse Riding

A premier horse riding destination for adults and children, from beginners to advanced — simple pony rides for the kids through to longer rides for the adventurous.

Thredbo Golf Course

Australia's highest golf course. Nine holes alongside the Thredbo River and around the base of the mountain, lined with eucalypts and wildflowers.

Thredbo Disc Golf

Eighteen holes nestled between bushland, open fields and eucalypts, surrounded by mountain views. Fun for all ages and abilities.

Rivers, Lakes & Caves

Water and limestone country

Fly Fishing

The pristine Thredbo River is home to some of the wildest trout in Australia. The season runs from the October long weekend to the June long weekend.

Gaden Trout Hatchery

On the Thredbo River near Jindabyne, one of Australia's main centres for breeding and rearing cold water sport fish.

Water Sports

Home to incredible alpine wilderness and Australia's highest peak, the Snowy Mountains present a stunning range of rivers, mountains and glacial lakes.

Yarrangobilly Caves

Explore six caves created from a belt of limestone laid down about 440 million years ago in Kosciuszko National Park.

Base yourself in the middle of it all

Chalets, cabins and sites at Sawpit Creek — 14 km from Perisher, minutes from Jindabyne.
